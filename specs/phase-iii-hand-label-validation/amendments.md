# Phase III Census Hand-Label Survivor Audit — Freeze and Amendment Log

**Status:** Draft. No revision is approved or frozen.

When the owner approves the resolved design, the initial freeze record will be appended
here with its date, Git-history anchor, design and question-answer digests, reason, and a
statement of every census, sampling, pilot, or label result visible at approval.

After the initial freeze, this is an append-only record. Existing entries may not be
edited, removed, or reordered. Every change must be a new numbered revision stating:

1. who approved it and when;
2. the Git-history anchor;
3. why the change was necessary;
4. whether it affects the claim, frame, sampling, evidence, labels, adjudication,
   analysis, or reporting;
5. the exact files and raw-byte SHA-256 digests governed by the revision; and
6. what frame counts, pilot findings, reviewer labels, adjudications, or estimates were
   visible when approval was given.

No amendment may use confirmatory outcomes to change the census criteria, evidence
standard, sample, label rules, stop gates, or primary estimands. If a post-label change is
unavoidable, the confirmatory study stops; a new prospective study must be designed with
new cases.
