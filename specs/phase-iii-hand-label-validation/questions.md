# Phase III Census Hand-Label Survivor Audit — Approval Questions

**Status:** Unresolved. These questions must be answered, recorded, and frozen before a
pilot is drawn or any confirmatory packet is opened. Silence is not approval.

## Claim and evidence standard

1. **Primary claim.** Do you approve the bounded claim—public-evidence substantiation and
   credible-support yield among frozen R15 survivors—or do you require a statutory gold
   standard? A statutory standard requires authoritative agency/contract-file
   confirmation and a separate design; public-source review cannot defensibly substitute
   for it.
2. **Firm-authored evidence.** May firm statements contribute to the `credible` category
   while remaining insufficient alone for `publicly_substantiated`, or must they be
   corroboration only for every reported category?
3. **Official designation.** Is explicit official use of “Phase III” required for
   `publicly_substantiated`, or may authoritative evidence of all four substantive
   elements establish that category without the label?

## Precision, budget, and sampling

4. **Confidence design.** What confidence level and target confidence-interval half-width
   should determine the probability-sample size?
5. **Review budget.** What maximum number of independently double-reviewed cases and
   adjudications is available? If this conflicts with the requested precision, the design
   stops and reports the attainable precision before sampling.
6. **Clustering.** Should the sample permit multiple relations for the same firm or target
   contract and use cluster-aware uncertainty, or select at most one relation per frozen
   cluster while preserving known positive inclusion probabilities and relation-level
   weights? The exact design must be fixed in advance.
7. **Pilot and sentinels.** Do you authorize a disjoint rubric pilot and blinded sentinel
   cases? If so, their sizes and composition must be approved before selection and they
   remain outside the confirmatory estimate.

## Reviewers and gates

8. **Qualifications.** What procurement, SBIR/STTR, technical, or legal experience is
   required for the two reviewers and third adjudicator?
9. **Independence.** Must reviewers be external to the project, or is a documented
   conflict-free internal reviewer acceptable? The R15 author cannot be the sole reviewer
   or adjudicator under either choice.
10. **Reliability statistic.** Which chance-corrected agreement measure accompanies raw
    agreement? The choice must precede labels because rare categories can make ordinary
    kappa misleading.
11. **Stop gates.** What minimum agreement and maximum unassessable/inaccessible rates
    must be met before interpreting substantiation yield? No numeric gate is inferred in
    this draft.
12. **Search budget.** What equal per-case time limit and permitted public repositories
    apply? Reviewers stop at the same frozen effort boundary even when a case looks
    promising.

## Disclosure and downstream use

13. **Case evidence.** Will case-level citations and evidence notes be public, public with
    redactions, or retained in a restricted audit artifact? The aggregate tables remain
    complete under every option.
14. **Downstream rule.** Do labels serve only to characterize the frozen census, as this
    draft requires? Any future criterion change must be a new prospective study with new
    data; these confirmatory labels cannot be used both to tune and to validate it.
15. **Later recall study.** If recall or total undercount remains a goal, do you authorize
    a separate feasibility design to obtain authoritative positives and verified
    negatives from agency-held records? This public-evidence survivor study cannot answer
    that question.
