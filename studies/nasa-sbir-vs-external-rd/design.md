# NASA SBIR Versus External Research Design

## Question

Compare NASA SBIR awards with other external NASA research and development projects.

## Estimand

Estimate an adjusted vector of outcome differences for projects with common mission topics.
Treat the first design as matched observational evidence.
Promote a causal claim only after finding an exogenous allocation rule.

## Cohorts

Match on topic, center, technology readiness level, start year, award size, duration, geography, and performer type.
Include firm age and pre-award outcomes when the performer is a firm.
Separate grants, contracts, and cooperative agreements.

## Outcomes

Measure mission adoption, follow-on procurement, operating status, licensing, non-SBIR revenue, productivity, capital, and exits.
Use patents and publications as intermediate outputs.
Use common observation windows and censor recent cohorts.

## Analysis

Publish overlap and balance before outcome estimates.
Use an event study when pre-award outcome histories exist.
Report the complete outcome vector instead of a composite score.

## Failure conditions

Do not compare unlike technology readiness levels as one mechanism effect.
Do not treat an absent procurement record as no adoption.
Do not call matched estimates causal.
